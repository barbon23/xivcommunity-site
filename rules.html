Code of Conduct

How we play, and how we treat each other.

These rules apply to all members across in-game, Discord, and any public space where you represent XIVCOMMUNITY. Last updated November 2026.

1. Behaviour and communication

  1. 1.1 Treat every member and opponent with basic respect. Trash talk during matches is fine; targeted harassment is not. Consequence: Warning, then kick.
  2. 1.2 No hate speech, slurs, or discriminatory language anywhere — in voice, text, or in-game chat. Consequence: Immediate kick, no appeal.
  3. 1.3 Do not post or share malicious links, NSFW content, or personal information of other members. Consequence: Immediate ban from Discord and clan.
  4. 1.4 Disputes between members should be raised with an officer, not in public channels. Consequence: Warning.

2. Activity and participation

  1. 2.1 Members must log in at least once every 14 days. Extended absences must be communicated to an officer in advance. Consequence: Kick after 14 days of silence.
  2. 2.2 If you accept a war squad slot, show up. Repeated no-shows without notice affect the whole team. Consequence: Removed from war squad rotation.
  3. 2.3 Recruits are on a 30-day trial. Active participation in at least two scrims is required for full member status. Consequence: Not promoted, slot released.

3. Fair play

  1. 3.1 No cheating, hacking, or use of third-party software that provides an unfair advantage. Consequence: Immediate permanent ban, no appeal.
  2. 3.2 Do not intentionally throw matches, including for rank manipulation purposes. Consequence: Warning, then kick.
  3. 3.3 Smurfing (playing on secondary accounts to deceive opponents) during official scrims is not allowed. Consequence: Disqualification from war squad.

4. Representing the clan

  1. 4.1 When you wear the [XIV] tag, you represent everyone in this clan. Conduct yourself accordingly in public lobbies.
  2. 4.2 Do not claim to speak for the clan leadership unless you are an officer or were explicitly authorised to do so. Consequence: Warning.

5. Appeals and enforcement

Officers make enforcement decisions. If you believe a decision was made in error, you may appeal through the support page. Appeals are reviewed by at least two officers and resolved within 72 hours. Decisions on rule 1.2, 1.3 and 3.1 violations are final.
